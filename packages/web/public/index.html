Check your medicines for interactions

Add the medicines you take, and MedGuard tells you which ones don't mix well — in plain words.

Add a medicine

Type a medicine name or its brand name, then press Add. You can use everyday spellings.

    Works offline — your list never leaves this device.

    MedGuard is an informational tool, not medical advice. It can miss interactions and does not know your dose, health history, or other factors. Always check with a pharmacist or doctor before changing how you take any medicine. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
