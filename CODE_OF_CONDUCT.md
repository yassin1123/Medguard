# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MedGuard to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to make
health information more accessible, regardless of experience, background, or
identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who rely on this tool.
- Treating data-accuracy concerns seriously and without ego.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, and dismissiveness
toward good-faith questions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Concerns can be raised with the maintainers via a private issue or the contact
listed in the repository. Reports will be handled discreetly and in good faith.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
